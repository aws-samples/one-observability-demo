# Wet vs Dry Food and Hydration

**Dry food (kibble)** is economical, convenient, storable, and its texture provides mild
dental abrasion. It is calorie-dense, so portion control matters.

**Wet food (canned)** is ~70–80% moisture, which supports hydration and urinary tract
health, is highly palatable, and helps pets feel full on fewer calories — useful for
weight control and for cats prone to urinary issues or that don't drink enough.

**Cats** especially benefit from wet food because they have a low thirst drive and are
prone to urinary crystals and kidney disease when chronically under-hydrated.

Many owners feed a **mix**: kibble for convenience and dental benefit plus wet food for
moisture and palatability. Whichever you choose, provide constant fresh water. Always
check that the food is labeled **complete and balanced** for the pet's life stage.
