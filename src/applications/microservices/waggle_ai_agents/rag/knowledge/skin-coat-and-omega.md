# Skin and Coat Health

A dull coat, dandruff, or excessive shedding often reflects diet. Skin and coat health
depend on adequate protein and specific fats.

Key nutrients:
- **Omega-3 fatty acids (EPA/DHA)** from fish oil reduce skin inflammation and improve
  coat shine; **omega-6 (linoleic acid)** maintains the skin barrier. A balanced omega-6
  to omega-3 ratio matters.
- **High-quality protein** — hair is mostly protein, so deficiency shows in the coat.
- **Zinc and biotin** support skin and hair.
- **Vitamin E and A** protect skin cells.

Foods marketed for "skin & coat" are enriched with these. Persistent skin issues can
also signal **food allergy** (itchy skin, ear infections) or parasites, so rule those
out. Improvements from diet typically take 6–8 weeks to become visible.
