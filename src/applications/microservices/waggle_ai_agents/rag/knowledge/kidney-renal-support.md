# Kidney (Renal) Support

Chronic kidney disease (CKD) is common in senior cats and dogs. Therapeutic renal diets
are proven to extend quality and length of life in diagnosed CKD.

Renal diet principles:
- **Reduced, high-quality protein** to lower the kidneys' waste-filtering load while
  still meeting needs. Do **not** over-restrict protein in early disease or in cats.
- **Low phosphorus** — the most important factor for slowing progression.
- **Omega-3 fatty acids** support kidney blood flow.
- **Increased palatability and moisture** (wet food) to fight the appetite loss and
  dehydration common in CKD.
- Added potassium and B vitamins, which are lost through increased urination.

Renal diets should only be used for pets **diagnosed** with kidney disease, under
veterinary guidance — they are not appropriate for healthy pets.
