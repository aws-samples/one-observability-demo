# Sensitive Stomach and Digestive Health

Dogs and cats with sensitive stomachs show vomiting, loose stools, gas, or reluctance
to eat. Common triggers include abrupt diet changes, high fat, and hard-to-digest
ingredients.

Dietary approaches:
- **Highly digestible, limited-ingredient diets** with a single novel protein reduce the
  variables that can irritate the gut.
- **Moderate, not excessive fat** — high-fat foods commonly worsen loose stools.
- **Prebiotic fiber** (beet pulp, chicory/inulin) and **probiotics** support healthy gut
  flora.
- **Easily digestible carbohydrates** like rice.
- Avoid rich table scraps and frequent treats.

Always transition foods gradually over 7–10 days. Persistent GI signs warrant a vet
visit to rule out parasites, infections, pancreatitis, or food allergy. A sensitive
stomach is different from a true food allergy — see the food allergy guidance.
