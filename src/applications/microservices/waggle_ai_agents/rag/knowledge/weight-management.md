# Weight Management and Obesity

Over half of dogs and cats are overweight. Excess weight shortens lifespan and worsens
diabetes, joint disease, and heart problems.

Dietary strategy:
- Feed a **weight-management formula**: lower calorie density, higher protein to preserve
  lean muscle, and higher fiber to promote satiety.
- **Measure portions** by weight, feed to the pet's *target* weight, not current weight.
- Cut treats to under 10% of daily calories; use pieces of the pet's kibble or
  vegetables (carrot, green beans) as low-calorie treats.
- Increase activity gradually.

Aim for gradual loss: about 1–2% of body weight per week for dogs, and no more than
0.5–1% for cats — **cats must not lose weight too fast** because rapid loss can cause
hepatic lipidosis (fatty liver), which is life-threatening. Monitor with regular weigh-ins.
