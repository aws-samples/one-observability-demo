# Joint Health and Mobility

Joint problems (arthritis, hip/elbow dysplasia) are common in senior dogs, large breeds,
and overweight pets. Nutrition can meaningfully support mobility.

Key nutrients:
- **Glucosamine and chondroitin** support cartilage.
- **Omega-3 fatty acids (EPA/DHA)** from fish oil reduce joint inflammation.
- **Green-lipped mussel** and **MSM** are common joint-support additives.
- **Maintaining a lean body weight** is the single most effective way to reduce joint
  strain — every extra pound adds load.

Many senior and large-breed formulas include these nutrients. For diagnosed arthritis,
veterinary therapeutic joint diets provide higher, clinically effective doses. Pair diet
with controlled, low-impact exercise.
