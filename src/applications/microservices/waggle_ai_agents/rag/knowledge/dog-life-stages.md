# Dog Nutrition by Life Stage

**Puppies (0–12 months, up to 24 for large breeds)** need calorie-dense food with
higher protein (22–32% dry matter) and controlled calcium/phosphorus for steady bone
growth. Feed puppy-formulated ("growth") diets; overfeeding large-breed puppies raises
the risk of skeletal disorders.

**Adult dogs (1–7 years)** need a balanced maintenance diet. Protein around 18–25%,
moderate fat, matched to activity level. Working or highly active dogs need more calories;
sedentary or neutered dogs need fewer to avoid weight gain.

**Senior dogs (7+ years, 6+ for large breeds)** benefit from lower-calorie diets to
prevent obesity as metabolism slows, high-quality easily digestible protein to preserve
muscle, and added omega-3 fatty acids and glucosamine/chondroitin for joint health.
Watch for reduced kidney function and adjust phosphorus accordingly.

Always transition between diets over 7–10 days to avoid digestive upset.
