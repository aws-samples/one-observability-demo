# Food Allergies and Intolerances

A true food allergy is an immune response to a protein. The most common canine food
allergens are **beef, dairy, chicken, wheat, and egg**; in cats, **beef, fish, and
dairy**. Signs are typically **itchy skin, recurrent ear infections, and paw licking**,
sometimes with GI upset.

Management:
- **Elimination diet** using a **novel protein** (venison, duck, rabbit, kangaroo) or a
  **hydrolyzed protein** diet where proteins are broken down so the immune system does
  not recognize them. Run it strictly for 8–12 weeks.
- **Limited-ingredient diets** reduce the number of potential triggers.
- Avoid treats, flavored medications, and table food during a trial.

Grain-free is not automatically better — most allergies are to animal proteins, not
grains. Diagnosis requires a controlled elimination trial, not blood tests alone.
Always involve a veterinarian.
